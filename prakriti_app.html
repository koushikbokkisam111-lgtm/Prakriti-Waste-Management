Problem Framing: India's Solid Waste Dilemma

🚨 The 22% Collection Leakage Gap

India produces ~1.7 lakh tonnes of Municipal Solid Waste daily. Approximately 54% is scientifically processed. But 22% (~37,400 tonnes/day) disappears due to last-mile collection leakages — dumped into storm drains, open plots, or burned illegally.
